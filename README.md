One sentence that could have replaced an entire pointless meeting: the decision is already in the line — yes, budget approved, deadline Friday — and the meeting is just that sentence, said slower, by six people, for an hour.

> The decision is yes, the budget is approved, and the deadline is Friday — this meeting is just that sentence, said slower, by six people, for an hour.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3 standard library only) writes the line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`, checked first against the competition gate: 1 to 200 characters of UTF-8 text, a single line.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
